> about corevalley

GPUs on demand, powered by the Himalayas.

CoreValley is Nepal's first hydro-powered GPU cloud. Spin up a notebook or a Kubernetes cluster in minutes — clean, affordable, and built for the region's AI builders.

$ think colab, but on nepali hydro
> why we exist

AI runs on compute, and compute runs on power. Nepal has some of the cleanest, most abundant hydropower in the world — yet teams across the region still rent GPUs from data centers a continent away, at prices and latencies set elsewhere.

We're changing that. CoreValley puts high-performance GPUs where the clean energy already is, so builders in Nepal and South Asia can train, fine-tune, and serve AI models on infrastructure that's local, green, and within reach.

> what we're building

Two ways to get compute, one platform:

$ gpu-as-a-service

Open a browser, pick a GPU, start coding. Hosted notebooks and on-demand instances for students, researchers, and startups — no setup, no hardware, pay for what you use.

$ kubernetes-as-a-service

Managed GPU clusters for teams that need to train at scale or run models in production. Bring your containers; we handle the infrastructure.

Both run on 100% renewable hydropower, with transparent pricing and support in your time zone.
